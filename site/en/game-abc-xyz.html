Game · Session 7

ABC × XYZ challenge

Classify 23 MBF items into ABC by value and XYZ by variability (beware the raw-CV trap of seasonal items), then choose a forecasting – inventory policy for each cell.

← All gamesSession 7
What you learn
  • Apply the ABC 80/95 rule and the XYZ thresholds
  • Understand why you must deseasonalise before measuring CV
  • Link each cell to a forecasting approach, a service level and planner time
Using it in class
  • The instructor shares the screen and plays one sample round, then groups of 3–5 play on their own machines and compare scores.
  • 5-minute discussion on the questions at the end of the game; links to Lecture Notes 7.1–7.2 · Session 7 slides.
  • Scores and records are stored only in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
