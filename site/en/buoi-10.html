S&OP meeting simulation & handover

Capstone presentations, surprise events and revealing MBF's 'future' answers

Stage 4 · SynthesisClass date: ILO8

By the end of this session you will be able to

  1. Present the 12-month demand plan as in a real S&OP meeting
  2. Handle a surprise event in 5 minutes: quantify, choose an option, state the trade-offs
  3. Answer the panel's challenging questions with data and assumptions
  4. Hand over the unconstrained demand plan to Production Planning
  5. Compare your forecast with MBF's 'future' and draw lessons
  6. Make a 30–60–90 day plan to apply at your company

Learning outcomes: ILO8 (integrating the full cycle, S&OP presentation, handover) · synthesis of ILO1–ILO7

While the other groups present

File: B10_MoPhong_SOP_Student.xlsx → Bai1_GopNhom, Bai2_SuKien

  1. Before presenting: paste each member's total revenue row into Bai1 — the group total
  2. While another group presents: write 1 challenge question for each group
  3. For each event card: quantify it yourself in Bai2 and compare with how the presenting group handled it
  4. Vote for 'the most convincing decision' (not graded, there's a prize)

Output: Bai1 group total + Bai2 quantifying the 4 events (submitted with the course feedback).

Company corner

MBF's future has arrived — We have the 'real' demand for 10/2026–09/2027 created by the data generator — whose forecast was closest?

The instructor and guests play roles — each asks questions from their own point of view.

RoleObjectiveOpening line
CEO (chair)One clear decision, with risks under control“In short, what do you need me to decide?”
Finance DirectorRevenue, gross margin, inventory capital“How many billion dong is this plan off budget?”
Sales DirectorEnough stock for the channels and the big customers“Why is your number lower than the target?”
Production DirectorA feasible, stable plan“In November I only have 89,800 cases.”
Production PlanningA complete handover file in the right format“Does this number include SS? Where does σ come from?”

After the course · A 30–60–90 day plan

  1. 30 days: measure current WMAPE, Bias and FVA for the 20 largest items (using the Lab or dpkit)
  2. 60 days: standardise the data adjustment log and the assumption log; run one Demand Review cycle with an agenda
  3. 90 days: an ABC/XYZ forecasting policy; safety stock from error σ; a 5-KPI report for S&OP
  4. Share your results with the ERX alumni group after 90 days
