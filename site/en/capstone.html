1. Context

It is early October 2026. You are the Demand Planner at Mekong Bloom Foods (MBF), responsible for a portfolio of 6 SKU × channel series. Data is locked up to the end of September 2026. Over the next three weeks you must run a full monthly forecasting cycle, take the results to the S&OP meeting, and hand over the October 2026–September 2027 demand plan to Production Planning.

The business questions you must answer
  • What is the unconstrained demand of the 6 series over the next 12 months — and how far can it be trusted?
  • Which business information (Tết 2027, approved promotions, events) should be layered onto the statistical forecast, and on what evidence?
  • How much safety stock is needed at which service level, and how much inventory capital does that tie up?
  • What are the three biggest risks/opportunities, and which decisions do you need leadership to make in the S&OP meeting?

2. Learning outcomes assessed

CodeLearning outcomes
ILO2Prepare demand data: detect errors and noise (stock-outs, end-of-period loading, returns, code changes, unit errors), restore unconstrained demand and keep an auditable adjustment log.
ILO3Build baseline forecasts (Naive, MA, SES, Holt) and evaluate them out of sample with suitable metrics (WMAPE, Bias, MASE), including by forecast lag.
ILO4Model seasonality, the Tết calendar effect, price and promotions; separate baseline from uplift and assess the financial return of promotions.
ILO5Design portfolio-level forecasting policies (ABC/XYZ, intermittent demand, aggregate levels) and select models on out-of-sample evidence.
ILO6Run a Demand Review: bring in information from every department, measure FVA and Tracking Signal, set adjustment rules and challenge with evidence.
ILO7Turn the forecast into operational decisions: safety stock from out-of-sample error, in-month early warning, shelf-life risk, capacity checks and value conversion for S&OP.
ILO8Complete a full monthly forecasting cycle, present an S&OP recommendation with trade-offs and hand over the unconstrained demand plan to Production Planning.

3.2 Option B — your own company's data

  • Register in HW1; submit an anonymised data description in HW7 for the instructor's approval (Pass/Revise gate).
  • Minimum requirements: 6 series, ≥ 24 months of monthly history, at least 1 seasonal or promoted series; cost/selling price available (may be scaled).
  • Anonymise as in section 6.3 of the course handbook: encode names, multiply by a fixed secret factor, round consistently; do not edit individual values; do not shift dates.
  • Include confirmation from your line manager that the data may be used for learning.
  • Same rubric as Option A; criterion 1 is graded on the real difficulty of the data you bring.

6. Group S&OP presentation (15%)

  • Groups of 3–5 combine their portfolios into one S&OP pack: total 12-month revenue, shared risks, decisions to be made.
  • 8-minute presentation + 4 minutes of Q&A in front of a panel (CEO, CFO, Sales Director, Production Director, Production Planning).
  • When the group starts presenting, the panel reveals one surprise event card; the group uses the last 5 minutes to quantify it and make a recommendation.
CardEvent

8. Frequently asked questions

QuestionAnswer
May I use the Lab or Python?Yes. But the final numbers must live in the workbook and be reproducible.
Is accuracy against the 'future' graded?No. In Session 10 the instructor reveals actual demand so the whole class learns from the error — the quality of your method and reasoning is what is graded.
Do I have to use demand_clean?Not required. Cleaning from sales_history yourself scores higher on criterion 1.
My series has no promotions?Say so in the report; criterion 3 is then graded on Tết, events and other business information.
May the report be in English?Yes, Vietnamese or English.
