Course text (Lecture Notes)

The main reading, 22 chapters. Part I (Chapters 1–10) follows the 10 sessions, with in-depth sections (Bullwhip, the Pegels matrix, ABC × XYZ) and “Advanced extensions” for material there is no time to cover in class. Part II (Chapters 11–19) covers advanced topics for self-study: probabilistic forecasting, ETS/ARIMA, hierarchical forecasting, machine learning, new products, causal effects of promotions, demand sensing, advanced inventory, process and maturity. Part III (Chapters 20–22): 11 company case studies, enterprise software and trends to 2030, automated reporting with Excel VBA · Power BI · AI. Every example uses the Mekong Bloom Foods case, with figures, code, review questions and suggested answers.

Term

TermMeaning in this course
Unconstrained demandWhat customers want to buy if not limited by inventory or capacity
GT / MTGeneral trade (distributors, grocery stores) / modern trade (supermarkets, chains)
Sell-in / sell-outCompany sales into distributors / distributor sales out to stores
Train / testThe data used to build the model / the data held back for out-of-sample evaluation (last 6 months)
WMAPEΣ|error| / Σ actual — the course's main metric
BiasΣ(forecast − actual) / Σ actual — positive means over-forecasting
MASEMAE of the model / in-sample MAE of Naive — < 1 beats the benchmark
LagThe gap between when a forecast is frozen and the month being forecast
FVAForecast Value Added — the error reduction contributed by a step in the process
Tracking SignalΣ error / MAD — beyond ±4 signals systematic bias
Safety Stockz × out-of-sample error σ × √lead time
FEFOFirst Expired, First Out — the lot that expires first ships first
S&OPSales & Operations Planning — the monthly process that aligns one plan
