How the homework works

Each assignment is a milestone of the capstone project: you work on your own portfolio (P1–P6 or approved company data) and gradually fill in Capstone_Template.xlsx. By Session 10 you will have most of a complete forecasting cycle.

WeightHW1–HW8: 30% of the course grade (3.75% each). HW9 is the final capstone, graded with the capstone rubric (45%).
SubmissionOn the LMS, before 23:59 on the due date (usually 2 days before the next session) so the instructor can give feedback in class.
GradingA shared 0–4 scale (below) plus a specific description for each assignment. General feedback at the start of the next session, individual feedback on the LMS within 5 working days.
LateUp to 24 hours: minus 1 level. After 24 hours: 0 points but you still get feedback.
ToolsExcel is required; Forecasting Lab and Python may be used to check or speed up — the final numbers live in the workbook.

Shared 0–4 scale

LevelDescription
4 · ExcellentCorrect and complete; can explain why; handles unclear points and validates the result (e.g. against benchmarks, out-of-sample checks). Could serve as a model answer.
3 · GoodCorrect in familiar situations and can explain the reasoning; small gaps that do not change the conclusion.
2 · PassMain parts done by following the guidance; some errors or loose reasoning but the conclusion is still usable.
1 · WeakSubmitted but mostly incorrect, incomplete or unexplained; the conclusion is not usable.
0 · Not submittedNot submitted, wrong assignment submitted, or a breach of academic integrity / data confidentiality.

HW1 · The forecasting map of my portfolio

SetAfter Session 1 · Why forecast demand & forecasting hierarchy
ObjectiveUnderstand your portfolio before forecasting: the shape of demand, variability at different levels and who uses which number.
Learning outcomesILO1
Capstone sheet01_Portfolio
Due23:59, 2 days before Session 2
Time needed≈ 2 hours
Submission1 Excel file + 1 paragraph (LMS)
Weight1/8 of 30% (HW1–HW8)

Tasks

  1. Take your assigned portfolio (P1–P6, 6 SKU × channel series) — or register to use anonymised company data
  2. Plot the 6 series (Lab pages for Sessions 1–2 or Excel); compute CV at series level and SKU level
  3. Build the department – decision map for these 6 series (use the Bai2_BanDo template)
  4. Write 150–200 words: 3 things that look 'odd' in the data and which questions you would ask whom

Grading criteria

LevelDescription for this assignment
4CV correct at both levels, explains why the higher level is more stable; a specific department – decision map for the 6 series; 3 sharp 'odd' points with questions sent to the right people
3CV correct; sensible map; comments have ideas but lack depth
2Small errors in CV; generic map
1CV or map missing; comments not tied to the data
0Not submitted or wrong assignment
Tips
  • Lab · the Session 1 page lets you quickly check results before filling in Excel.
  • The B01 Solution file (released after class) shows how to present — it is not the answer for your portfolio.

After the course · 30–60–90 day plan (not graded)

An optional exercise to take the forecasting cycle back to your company; share it with the ERX alumni group after 90 days.

  • 30 days: measure current WMAPE, Bias and FVA for the 20 largest items (using the Lab or dpkit)

  • 90 days: an ABC/XYZ forecasting policy; safety stock from error σ; a 5-KPI report for S&OP
