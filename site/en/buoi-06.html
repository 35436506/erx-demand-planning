Regression, price and promotions

Putting promotions into the forecast and challenging Marketing with numbers

Stage 2 · Core techniquesClass date: ILO4

By the end of this session you will be able to

  1. Run multiple regression with LINEST/Data Analysis and read the coefficients, R², p-values
  2. Choose explanatory variables that are KNOWN IN ADVANCE at the time of forecasting
  3. Split the forecast into baseline + uplift; estimate the post-promotion dip
  4. Test Marketing's proposal with the model and with gross margin
  5. Understand cannibalisation between SKUs and the limits of monthly data
  6. Forecast new products: channel fill + repeat sales

Learning outcomes: ILO4 (price, promotions, cannibalisation, NPD) · ILO6 (challenging Marketing's information)

Key points to remember
  • Regression brings causes into the forecast — only with variables known in advance
  • Separate baseline and uplift; explain coefficients in business language
  • A promotion that lifts volume does not necessarily lift profit — always compute gross margin
