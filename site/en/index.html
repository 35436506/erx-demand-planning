How to get the most out of this website

Before the session
  • Read the session objectives and further reading
  • Download the Student Excel file
  • Submit the previous session's homework
During the session
  • Follow the slides in the viewer
  • Open the session's Lab page when the instructor demos
  • Complete the 5-question exit ticket at the end
After the session
  • Do the homework = a capstone milestone
  • Tick off your progress and record your scores
  • Review with the Lecture Notes

By the end you will be able to

  • ILO1 — Explain the role of forecasting in the planning system (S&OP → MPS → MRP), distinguish demand, forecast, plan and target, and choose the right forecasting level for each decision.
  • ILO2 — Prepare demand data: detect errors and noise (stock-outs, end-of-period loading, returns, code changes, unit errors), restore unconstrained demand and keep an auditable adjustment log.
  • ILO3 — Build baseline forecasts (Naive, MA, SES, Holt) and evaluate them out of sample with suitable metrics (WMAPE, Bias, MASE), including by forecast lag.
  • ILO4 — Model seasonality, the Tết calendar effect, price and promotions; separate baseline from uplift and assess the financial return of promotions.
  • ILO5 — Design portfolio-level forecasting policies (ABC/XYZ, intermittent demand, aggregate levels) and select models on out-of-sample evidence.
  • ILO6 — Run a Demand Review: bring in information from every department, measure FVA and Tracking Signal, set adjustment rules and challenge with evidence.
  • ILO7 — Turn the forecast into operational decisions: safety stock from out-of-sample error, in-month early warning, shelf-life risk, capacity checks and value conversion for S&OP.
  • ILO8 — Complete a full monthly forecasting cycle, present an S&OP recommendation with trade-offs and hand over the unconstrained demand plan to Production Planning.
