Beer Game · The bullwhip effect

MIT's classic beer distribution game: you run one stage of a 4-stage chain and only see the orders from the stage below. Experience the bullwhip, then play again with sell-out data shared.

← All gamesSession 1Session 8
What you learn
  • See for yourself how the bullwhip arises from sensible ordering rules at each stage
  • Understand the role of goods in transit (the supply line) and lead time
  • Compare chain cost when sell-out data is shared
Using it in class
  • The instructor shares the screen and plays one sample round, then groups of 3–5 play on their own machines and compare scores.
  • 5-minute discussion on the questions at the end of the game; links to Lecture Notes 1.4–1.5 · Chapter 20 (P&G, Barilla).
  • Scores and records are stored only in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
