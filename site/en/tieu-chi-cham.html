Shared 0–4 scale

Level 3 means “can handle familiar situations independently and explain why” — the course target for every skill.

LevelDescription
4 · ExcellentCorrect and complete; can explain why; handles unclear points and validates the result (e.g. against benchmarks, out-of-sample checks). Could serve as a model answer.
3 · GoodCorrect in familiar situations and can explain the reasoning; small gaps that do not change the conclusion.
2 · PassMain parts done by following the guidance; some errors or loose reasoning but the conclusion is still usable.
1 · WeakSubmitted but mostly incorrect, incomplete or unexplained; the conclusion is not usable.
0 · Not submittedNot submitted, wrong assignment submitted, or a breach of academic integrity / data confidentiality.

Submission rules, integrity and AI

  • Up to 24 hours late: minus 1 level. After 24 hours: 0 points but you still get feedback.
  • Homework and the capstone are individual work; cite sources in Harvard style.
  • Do not optimise parameters on test data or “peek” at future data — the “Model evaluation” criterion is capped at 1/4 if you do.
  • You may use AI to ask about concepts, suggest formulas, write code or polish wording — declare it in one line at the end of the report and verify every number yourself.
  • Do not put real, non-anonymised company data into public AI tools.
