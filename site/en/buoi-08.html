Cross-functional collaboration & Demand Review

FVA, Tracking Signal and a 60-minute Demand Review meeting game

Stage 3 · Process & decisionsClass date: ILO6

By the end of this session you will be able to

  1. Describe what information each department brings to the forecast and which way it tends to be biased
  2. Measure Forecast Value Added (FVA): which adjustments help and which hurt
  3. Use the Tracking Signal to detect systematic bias
  4. Set adjustment rules: who, when, how much, what to record
  5. Chair a Demand Review meeting and agree the consensus number
  6. Keep an assumption log for the capstone project

Learning outcomes: ILO6 (Demand Review, FVA, challenging with evidence)

Company corner

Game: a 60-minute Demand Review meeting — 5 roles · 3 scenario cards · one consensus number.

Each role has its own goal — and one piece of information the others don't have.

RoleObjectiveOpening line
Demand Planner (chair)Agree one unconstrained consensus number + assumptions“Let's go through each card, 12 minutes per card.”
Sales GTHave enough stock for the big distributor, don't lose the customer“The Mekong Delta distributor will definitely take an extra 6,000 cases.”
MarketingAdd a December promotion for N02“20% off in MT, at least a 60% lift.”
Supply / ProductionDon't exceed November capacity (maintenance)“November has only ~82,000 cases of packet noodles.”
FinanceA number that matches the Q4 budget, gross margin not falling“Who pays for the extra promotion?”

Exit ticket · self-check

Answer in the last 2–3 minutes of the session. The instructor goes through the answers in class — record how many you got right.

  1. The FVA of the 'Sales' step = −5 % points. What does that mean?
  2. Why does consensus help a lot in promotion months but hurt in months without promotions?
  3. Tracking Signal = −6. Is the forecast running high or low against actuals?
  4. Supply reports a capacity shortfall. Should the consensus demand number be reduced to match?
  5. The three required pieces of information in an assumption log entry.

Key points to remember
  • Every adjustment step must answer: is it better than the previous step (FVA)?
  • Adjust when you have information, not on gut feeling — and record the assumptions
  • Demand Review agrees UNCONSTRAINED demand; capacity is Supply Review's job
